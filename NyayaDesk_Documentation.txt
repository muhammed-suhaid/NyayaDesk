# NyayaDesk – Legal Case Management System
## Complete Project Documentation for Presentation

---

### 1. Project Title
**NyayaDesk – Legal Case Management System**
*A modern, AI-integrated digital solution for law firms and legal practitioners.*

---

### 2. Abstract
Legal case management has traditionally relied on manual record-keeping, leading to data silos, document misplacement, and delayed proceedings. **NyayaDesk** is a comprehensive Legal Case Management System (LCMS) designed to streamline law firm operations. By centralizing case details, client information, advocate tasks, and hearing schedules, the system ensures high organizational efficiency. A standout feature of NyayaDesk is its integration with **Google Gemini AI**, which provides intelligent case analysis and specialized legal assistance focused on Indian Law. This project bridge the gap between traditional legal practice and modern technology.

---

### 3. Introduction
**Background of Legal Case Management:**
The legal profession involves managing vast amounts of data, including case files, hearing dates, client records, and legal research. Traditionally, this was done via physical registers and folders.

**Problems Faced Without Digital Systems:**
*   **Difficulty in Tracking:** Searching for case history or upcoming hearing dates is time-consuming.
*   **Document Clutter:** Physical documents are difficult to organize, search, and store securely.
*   **Communication Gaps:** Poor coordination between senior advocates, juniors, and clients regarding case status.
*   **Human Error:** Missed hearing dates or deadlines can lead to severe legal consequences.

**Importance of Case Management Software:**
Digital systems like NyayaDesk provide a single source of truth. They automate repetitive tasks, ensure data integrity, and offer analytical insights (AI-powered) that were previously impossible with manual systems.

---

### 4. Objectives of the Project
The primary goal is to provide a unified platform for law firms to manage their entire lifecycle efficiently. Specific objectives include:
*   **Centralized Management:** Efficiently manage Advocates, Clients, and Cases under one roof.
*   **Proactive Tracking:** Track hearing dates, case updates, and document history in real-time.
*   **Enhanced Document Organization:** Digital storage and management of case-related files.
*   **Operational Control:** Attendance and leave tracking for administrative management of law firm staff.
*   **AI-Driven Intelligence:** Provide AI-powered legal analysis and a specialized legal assistant for research.

---

### 5. Scope of the System
**Where it can be used:**
*   **Law Firms:** Multi-advocate firms requiring collaborative case management.
*   **Individual Practitioners:** Independent lawyers needing a digital office assistant.
*   **Legal Departments:** Corporate legal wings for internal case tracking.

**Limitations:**
*   Currently localized database (SQLite) for direct deployments; requires migration for massive-scale cloud setups.
*   Initial focus is primarily on Indian legal procedures and statutes.

---

### 6. Technology Stack
**Frontend (Client Side):**
*   **React:** For building a dynamic and responsive Single Page Application (SPA).
*   **Material UI (MUI):** For a clean, professional, and accessible user interface components.
*   **React Router:** Handles seamless navigation across multiple pages.
*   **Axios:** Manages robust API communication with the backend.
*   **Recharts:** Used for data visualization in reports and analytics.

**Backend (Server Side):**
*   **Python (Flask):** A lightweight yet powerful REST API framework.
*   **Flask-SQLAlchemy:** ORM (Object Relational Mapper) for database interactions.
*   **ReportLab:** For generating professional PDF reports and invoices.

**Database:**
*   **SQLite:** A serverless, self-contained SQL database engine ideal for local law firm environments.

**AI Integration:**
*   **Google Gemini AI:** Utilizes state-of-the-art Generative AI for case summarization and legal consultations.

---

### 7. System Architecture
The system follows a **Client-Server Architecture**:

1.  **Frontend (React):** Captures user input and displays data. It sends HTTP requests (JSON) via Axios.
2.  **Backend (Flask):** The logic layer. It receives requests, validates authentication (JWT/RBAC), and interacts with the DB or AI service.
3.  **Database (SQLite):** Stores persistent data such as user credentials, case facts, and hearing logs.
4.  **AI Service (Gemini):** The backend communicates with Google's Gemini API for processing legal logic and returning analysis.

**Data Flow:**
User Input → React Component → API Endpoint → Flask Route → SQLAlchemy Model → Database → Response back to UI.

---

### 8. Modules of the System
*   **Authentication Module:** Secure login system with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensuring users only see what they are authorized to.
*   **Super Admin Module:** System-wide management, including managing different law firm "Companies" and billing.
*   **Admin Module:** Firm-level control; managing the roster of advocates and firm-wide configurations.
*   **Case Management Module:** The core module for creating, editing, and tracking the lifecycle of legal cases.
*   **Client Management Module:** Maintains a directory of clients, their contact details, and their associated cases.
*   **Advocate Management Module:** Tracks employee details, expertise, and assigned workloads.
*   **Attendance & Leave Module:** HR functionality to track staff presence and manage time-off requests.
*   **Document Management Module:** Securely uploads and organizes case-specific legal documents and evidence.
*   **Notification Module:** System alerts for upcoming hearings, task deadlines, and case updates.
*   **Reports Module:** Generates analytics on case distributions, success rates, and staff performance.

---

### 9. AI Features
*   **AI Case Analysis:** Advocates input case facts and titles. The AI analyzes this data to suggest:
    *   Legal summaries and key arguments.
    *   Relevant Indian laws and statutes.
    *   Evidence strategies and potential line of questioning.
*   **AI Legal Assistant:** A specialized chat interface where advocates can ask complex questions about Indian Law (IPC, BNS, etc.) and receive instant, structured explanations.

---

### 10. Database Design
**Main Database Tables:**
*   **Users:** Stores login credentials, roles (Super Admin, Admin, Advocate), and contact info.
*   **Companies:** Stores information about the law firms using the system.
*   **Advocates:** Profiles of legal professionals linked to a company.
*   **Clients:** Details of individuals or entities seeking legal representation.
*   **Cases:** Core table storing Case No, Court, Facts, and Status.
*   **Hearings:** Logs of every court appearance date and associated notes.
*   **CaseUpdates:** History of changes and milestones in a case.
*   **Documents:** Meta-data and file paths for uploaded case files.
*   **Attendance & LeaveRequests:** Records for staff administrative management.
*   **Payments:** Tracking billing and financial transactions.

**Relationships:**
*   Each **Case** belongs to one **Company** and is linked to multiple **Clients** and **Advocates**.
*   **Hearings** and **Documents** are children of a specific **Case**.

---

### 11. API Endpoints
*   **Auth APIs:** `/api/auth/login`, `/api/auth/register` (handles session security).
*   **Case APIs:** `/api/cases/` (CRUD operations for legal cases).
*   **AI APIs:** `/api/ai/case-analysis` (Gemini analysis) and `/api/ai/legal-question` (Q&A).
*   **Reports APIs:** `/api/reports/` (Statistics and PDF generation).
*   **HR APIs:** `/api/attendance/` and `/api/leave-requests/`.

---

### 12. User Roles
*   **Super Admin:** The system owner. Can manage all law firms (companies), view global summary, and handle billing.
*   **Admin (Firm Owner):** Can manage advocates and clients within their firm, view firm reports, and configure firm settings.
*   **Advocate (Staff):** Can manage their assigned cases, upload documents, track their own hearings, and use AI features for research.

---

### 13. User Interface
*   **Dashboard:** High-level overview of active cases, upcoming hearings, and firm stats.
*   **Cases Page:** Categorized list (Active/Closed) with powerful search filters.
*   **Case Details:** A 360-degree view of a case including facts, documents, hearings, and updates.
*   **AI Assistant Page:** A clean, chat-focused interface for legal research and analysis.
*   **Client/Advocate Directories:** User-friendly management of contacts.
*   **Reports Page:** Visual charts and downloadable CSV/PDF reports.

---

### 14. Security Features
*   **RBAC (Role-Based Access Control):** Strict isolation of data between different law firms and user levels.
*   **Input Validation:** Prevention of SQL injection and malicious script uploads.
*   **API Security:** All endpoints are protected via authentication guards.
*   **Error Handling:** Graceful failure management to ensure system stability even during network issues.
